## Why?

AI is spreading everywhere, and I want a place where I can store my scripts and share them publicly without worrying about an AI writing over my work. That is why I'll have PRs and issues off, but if you'd like to reach me for suggestions or changes, I will not take spoon-fed code but would love to hear your feedback on how things are working for you.
